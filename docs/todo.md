- Index stock price = weighted average of all stocks weighted by their market cap
- sometimes we don't have any intuition on any one stock but we have feeling that majority of them will go up or down
- Index stock helps here. basically doing the weighted average of movements

2. CASH REDEEM 
- This card directy helps to redeem cash in your turn

3. CENTRAL BANK RATE CUT 
- players holding cash will get aggregated interest from all these cards

4. GLOBAL RECESSION 
- treat this as -1 for all stocks

5. TECHNOLOGY BOOM 
- treat this like +1 for all stocks

6. DIVIDEND 
- this is stock specific card. All thhose stock holders will get cash of 5% oh their holdings

7. RESTORE THE CLOSED COMPANY 
- this will restore the company price back to when it was last time (or base price). bringing back all movement cards from next cycle

8. CHARIMAN
- player holding more than 50% of total shares of a company makes him chairman
- In open phase, chairman can discard any 1 movement card
